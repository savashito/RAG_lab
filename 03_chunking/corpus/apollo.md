The Apollo program was the United States human spaceflight effort that landed the first people on the Moon. Apollo 11 launched on July 16, 1969, carrying astronauts Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin, and Michael Collins. Four days later, the lunar module Eagle touched down on a flat plain called the Sea of Tranquility. Neil Armstrong then became the first person to step onto the Moon, calling it one small step for man. In total, twelve astronauts walked on the lunar surface across six successful landings. The program ended with the final mission, Apollo 17, in December 1972.
